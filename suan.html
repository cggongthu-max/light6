[Sakura’s quick-sum scheme]

Group the addidends into, compute each group sum,

then apply the grouping again.

= (1+7)+(21+23)+(34+37)

=8+44+71

=(8+44)+71

=52+71

[Sakura’s carry borrow scheme]

To subtract hours of time to previous day, borrow 24 hours first for subtraction.

Example: Convert 7AM (Shanghai, UTC+8) to San Francisco (PT, UTC-8)

UTC-8=7AM-16=(7+24)-16 = 15 o’clock from previous day.
